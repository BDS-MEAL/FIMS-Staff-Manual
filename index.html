Contents

1. Introduction to FIMS

The Field Issue Management System (FIMS) helps us identify, monitor, manage and follow up issues found during MEAL routine monitoring visits.

FIMS Dashboard

Data insights and visual summaries of monitoring visits and the issues identified by the MEAL team.

FIMS Mobile App

Lets relevant staff review identified issues, provide updates, and mark issues as Closed or Escalated.

The two are connected. When you update an issue in the app, the change appears in the dashboard.

2. FIMS Dashboard

The dashboard gives you insights from MEAL routine monitoring visits. Through it you can:

  • View monitoring information relevant to your work
  • Filter records by relevant criteria
  • View issues identified by the MEAL Department
  • Find issues for a particular intervention, district, region or staff member
  • Monitor the current status of identified issues
  • Review overall trends and patterns from MEAL monitoring

Open it from the FIMS Mobile App or directly with this link:

FIMS Dashboardhttps://meal-dashboard.streamlit.app/
Use a laptop or tablet for the dashboard. The larger screen gives a better viewing experience.

4. Installing Power Apps on your phone

1

Search for Power Apps

Open the Google Play Store (Android) or the App Store (iOS) and search for Microsoft Power Apps.

2

Install Power Apps

Select the official Microsoft Power Apps app and install it.

3

Open Power Apps

The Power Apps icon appears on your phone. Open the app.

4

Open the login screen

After opening Power Apps, you will see the login screen.

5

Sign in with your office email

Select Sign in and use your official office email address.

Important
  • Always use your official BDS office email
  • Do not use a personal email address
  • FIMS access is linked to your office account

Example: if your name is Harshanath, your account is Harshanath@bds.berendina.org

Depending on your account security settings, you may be asked to verify your identity with a Mobile OTP or the Microsoft Authenticator app.

7. Reviewing and managing open issues

After you open the records, the Open Issues section shows:

  • The interventions visited by the MEAL team
  • Issues identified during MEAL monitoring visits
  • The current status of each issue

If an issue has been identified, the responsible staff member should review it and take action. An issue can be:

Closed

The issue has been addressed or resolved.

Escalated

The issue cannot be resolved at the current level or needs further attention.

8. Closing an issue

If you have taken the necessary action, mark the issue as Closed.

1

Select the green arrow

Tap the green arrow next to the issue.

2

Review the issue details

Read the details before you continue.

3

Enter a comment

Explain the action you took.

4

Submit the update

The issue leaves Open Issues and appears under Closed Issues.

Example. The MEAL team finds that fewer participants attended an activity than planned. If corrective action is possible, the responsible staff member acts on it and records an update in FIMS.

9. Escalating an issue

Some issues cannot be resolved at the current stage. For example, the activity may already be complete, so immediate corrective action is no longer possible. In that case, escalate the issue.

1

Select the red cross (X)

Tap the red cross next to the issue.

2

Explain the situation

Write a comment describing why you are escalating.

3

Submit the update

The issue leaves Open Issues and is recorded under Escalated Issues.

Important. Escalating does not mean ignoring the issue. Take steps to prevent the same issue in future activities.

10. Viewing closed and escalated issues

Closed and escalated issues leave the Open Issues section. Find them under Closed Issues and Escalated Issues, using the tabs at the top of the app.

To reopen an issue, select the back arrow next to the record. It returns to Open Issues.

11. Background and location of an issue

Select the arrow next to a record to see:

  • Background information about the issue
  • Additional information recorded during the MEAL visit
  • The beneficiary or intervention location, where available
  • Additional observations made by the MEAL team
A green dot next to the arrow means the MEAL Executive has recorded additional observations for that record.

17. Guidelines for FIMS users

Use your official office account

Always use your BDS office email to access FIMS.

Review issues regularly

Check the app often for new issues relevant to your responsibilities.

Provide meaningful comments

When you close or escalate, describe the action taken or the reason for escalation.

Take corrective action where possible

Address issues whenever correction is possible.

Prevent repeated issues

Even if an activity is already complete, use the lessons to avoid similar issues in future activities.

Keep information accurate

Make sure every update you enter is accurate and relevant.

Use the dashboard for decisions

Review monitoring information, spot patterns and understand issues in your work area.

18. How FIMS works

MEAL routine monitoring visit
Issues identified by MEAL
Issues recorded in FIMS
Staff review issues in the FIMS app
Staff take corrective action or escalate
Status updated in FIMS
FIMS dashboard updated
Management and staff review insights

FIMS links MEAL monitoring, issue management, corrective action and data-driven decision-making in one continuous loop.
